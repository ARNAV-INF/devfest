## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory intimacy and calm rhythm of an artisanal neighborhood cafe. Rooted in tactile organic warmth, it pairs traditional editorial serenity with approachable humanist modernity. The interface feels like natural linen stationery, unglazed ceramic dishware, and sunlit wood surfaces.

The visual style blends soft contemporary minimalism with subtle craft touches:
- **Atmosphere:** Unhurried, welcoming, fragrant, and grounded.
- **Audience:** Discerning coffee lovers, mindful morning ritualists, neighborhood regulars, and remote creatives who seek focus and ease.
- **Stylistic Pillars:** Gentle cream surfaces replace harsh whites; sage botanicals guide interactive flow; blush accents bring warmth to status and highlights; espresso-tinted typography provides soft, high-contrast readability without sterile black inks.

## Colors

The color palette is built around warm roasted undertones, herbal botanicals, and delicate bakery pastels. Pure digital black (#000000) and optical white (#FFFFFF) are strictly avoided to sustain an organic, paper-like tactile feel.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#7D9D85`):** Muted sage green. Drives primary actions, active navigation states, and confirmed selections.
- **Primary Deep (`#5B7A63`):** Deep roasted sage for hover states, focused focus rings, and high-emphasis botanical headers.
- **Secondary (`#E8B4B8`):** Dusty rose blush for promotional badges, seasonal coffee tags, favoriting, and gentle decorative accents.
- **Secondary Soft (`#F4DCD6`):** Warm milk-foam blush for card backdrops, subtle highlight fills, and alert banners.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`):** Steamed milk cream providing a non-glare, warm background surface.
- **Canvas Container (`#F5EFEB`):** Light unbleached linen surface for cards, floating menus, and layered content blocks.
- **Border Subtle (`#E8E1D7`):** Low-contrast organic divider hue matching unglazed earthenware.

### Text & Contrast Hierarchy
- **Espresso Dark (`#2D2926`):** Dark roasted bean tone for headlines, primary copy, and strong interactive states. Exceeds WCAG AAA requirements on cream.
- **Espresso Medium (`#4A443F`):** Secondary text, timestamps, brewing parameters, and body subtitles.
- **Warm Muted (`#7C736D`):** Inactive icons, placeholder prompts, input labels, and subtle meta-notes.

## Typography

The typographic voice unites the literary elegance of **Newsreader** with the soft, clear friendliness of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs:** Newsreader handles all major storytelling nodes, menu item names, and origin notes. It imparts an authentic, artisanal bookishness. Italic cuts may be used sparingly for flavor profile tasting notes (e.g., *notes of bergamot and honeycomb*).
- **Functional Humanist Sans:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers warm clarity for micro-copy, ordering options, pricing, preparation instructions, and interactive UI controls.
- **Rhythm & Line Heights:** Generous leading maintains an open, quiet breathing room across all reading views.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 8pt baseline rhythm integrated into a warm, open grid layout. Layouts avoid dense tabular arrangements in favor of gallery-inspired compositions.

### Grid Specifications
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) safe margins.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) page margins.
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Vertical Rhythm & Pacing
- Component interiors rely on compact padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).
- Sections and editorial feature modules are separated by ample negative space (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`), honoring the unhurried spirit of the brand.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile ceramic layering, soft tonal differentiation, and sun-warmed ambient shadows instead of sharp industrial drops.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Base):** `#FAF7F2` unbleached paper backdrop. Zero shadow.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** `#F5EFEB` with a micro-border `1px solid #E8E1D7` and an ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover / Active State):** Smooth transition upward: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Modals & Cart Flyout):** Surface rendered in `#FAF7F2` backed by `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.12)`.
- **Backdrop Scrim:** Semi-translucent espresso wash: `rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.35)` with an ultra-light `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 3 (Pill-shaped)** roundedness, celebrating the organic curves of ceramic mugs, river-smoothed pebbles, and steam swirls.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Pills & Badges:** `9999px` full round caps for status badges, filter tags, pill buttons, and quantity counters.
- **Cards & Containers:** `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (20px-24px) corner radii for beverage cards, tasting logs, and modal sheets.
- **Interactive Form Inputs:** `1rem` (16px) corner radii for text fields and steppers.
- **Avatars & Image Cutouts:** Soft arched silhouettes or fully rounded shapes with soft borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid muted sage `#7D9D85` background with `#FAF7F2` typography, full pill radius (`9999px`), horizontal padding `1.75rem`, vertical padding `0.75rem`. Hover smoothly shifts to deep sage `#5B7A63` accompanied by a gentle `scale(1.015)` spring transformation.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped outline with `1.5px solid #7D9D85`, transparent fill, and `#2D2926` text. Hover fills with `#F4DCD6` (milk blush) while retaining the sage border.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Borderless, `#4A443F` text with an underline offset of 4px in sage. Hover shifts text to `#2D2926`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Neutral / Inactive:** Background `#F5EFEB`, border `1px solid #E8E1D7`, text `#4A443F`. Full pill shape.
- **Active / Selected:** Background `#7D9D85`, border `1px solid #7D9D85`, text `#FAF7F2`, accompanied by an optional small check icon.
- **Accent Badges (Seasonal / Single Origin):** `#F4DCD6` background with `#2D2926` text and an optional `#E8B4B8` border.

### Product & Story Cards
- **Construction:** Structured with `#F5EFEB` background, `20px` border radius, and a hairline `1px solid #E8E1D7` rim. Internal padding is `1.25rem` to `1.5rem`.
- **Card Media:** Images are inset with `12px` rounded corners, warm tone-mapped photography, and subtle zoom on card hover.
- **Pricing & Actions:** Displayed on an intrinsic bottom flex row with espresso price tags and a rounded mini-stepper or pill-add button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1.5px solid #E8E1D7`, border-radius `1rem`, text `#2D2926`. 
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#7D9D85` with an outer glow box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(125, 157, 133, 0.2)`.
- **Labels:** Rendered in `label-md` using `#4A443F`, anchored 6px above the field.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** `6px` rounded square, `1.5px solid #7C736D` when unselected. When checked, filled with `#7D9D85` featuring a crisp cream checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Pure circle, outer ring `1.5px solid #7C736D`. Checked state features a centered `#7D9D85` filled pip with a `3px` white space buffer.

### Custom Cafe Components
- **Origin & Roast Meter:** A horizontal 5-bead indicator displaying roast depth (Light to Dark) using filled sage circles and empty cream rings.
- **Tasting Note Tag Cloud:** Pill badges in `#FAF7F2` with subtle italicized Newsreader typography, wrapped in a dashed `#E8B4B8` border.
- **Brew Timer / Steeper Widget:** Circular progress track in `#F5EFEB` with a smooth stroke in `#7D9D85` and real-time haptic-styled countdown numbering.
